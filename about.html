Meet the City Family

Atlanta's Exotic Animal & Ranch Experience

Our Story

City Boy Petz started with a passion for animals and a mission to bring joy, education, and connection to communities across metro Atlanta. What began as a local exotic animal showcase has grown into a full ranch experience — The City Family Ranch — offering horseback riding, private events, and unforgettable encounters with nature.

Our Mission

"Where the City Meets the Wild."

We believe every child deserves a chance to connect with the natural world, and every event deserves to be extraordinary. From exotic reptiles to majestic horses, we bring people and animals together in safe, supervised, and unforgettable ways.

Meet Our Animals

Exotic Reptiles

From ball pythons and bearded dragons to monitor lizards and tortoises — our reptiles are healthy, well-cared-for, and handler-socialized for safe guest interactions.

Farm Animals

Friendly goats, bunnies, and other farm favorites that kids and adults love to meet up close. Perfect for petting zoo experiences at any event.

Horses

Our ranch horses are well-trained and calm, available for guided trail rides, photoshoots, pony parties, and wedding photography at The City Family Ranch.

The Story of CityBoyPetz

Who Are We

CityBoyPetz is a family-built business founded by a couple who came together through a shared passion and genuine love for animals. What began as a mutual love for animals grew into a vision of creating extraordinary experiences and turning people's imaginations into reality.

From petting zoos and horse and pony parties to unforgettable wedding experiences, birthday celebrations, maternity sessions, and professional photo and video shoots, we bring something truly special to every occasion. We believe every vision deserves the opportunity to come to life, and our animals help create those once-in-a-lifetime moments that people will cherish forever.

Whether we're adding magic to a child's birthday, elegance to a wedding, or a unique touch to a creative production, we take pride in making every experience extraordinary. Built on family, dedication, creativity, and a whole lot of love for what we do, CityBoyPetz is committed to going beyond expectations. We don't just bring animals to your events — we bring your vision to life, one extraordinary experience at a time.

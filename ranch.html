The City Family Ranch

Nestled in McDonough, Georgia, The City Family Ranch offers a stunning private venue for weddings, receptions, baby showers, birthdays, family reunions, graduations, engagement sessions, and corporate gatherings. With scenic landscapes, exotic animals, and horses on-site, there is simply no other venue like it in Metro Atlanta.

Venue Features

Outdoor Ceremony Space

Beautiful open-air settings with natural backdrops, flexible layouts, and ample space for gatherings of all sizes.

Animal Encounters

Add a truly unique element to your event — exotic animals, petting zoo experiences, or horseback photo opportunities.

Scenic Photography

Rolling landscapes, rustic ranch aesthetics, horses, and golden-hour lighting that make every photo breathtaking.
